# 37: Readable cards and the "AI played" banner

**What to build:** Owner feedback after a game against the AI (ticket 36): card names alone mean looking every card up. Every face-up card in the Advisor Window (pyramid, cities, discard pile, Wonders, Progress tokens, draft/token/Library/Statue/Messe/Mausoleum tiles, the name pickers, the card menu) shows its cost and effect as icons (one emoji per resource and effect kind, with an icon key), the chain it comes from, and on the pyramid the price for the player to move right now (`Payment`, trading included). Hover (or tap) shows the full details: name, type, Age, full cost, effect in plain words, chain in/out, and both players' price breakdown. A big "AI played" banner at the top of the board names the AI's Moves since the owner's last Move ("AI built Caravansery for 2🪙", "AI built The Pyramids using Glassworks for 0🪙", "AI took Progress token: Law") until the owner moves, and highlights the slot the card came from. All text comes from the card data (no per-card text in the page); nothing hidden is named.
