## Notes

- **Reason tags** (`Advisor/Reasons.cs`): the Move (or Statue pair) is applied to a copy of the owner's Observation and the before/after board signals of `DerivedFeatures` are compared: completed science pairs ("completes your quill pair: you pick a Progress token") and new symbols (also the opponent's, for a given Statue card), the pawn ("+2 shields: pawn 3 from their capital") and military tokens, VP without the treasury, coins/costs, production gained or destroyed, trading prices that change, flexible resources, chains used and opened ("chain: Observatory free later"), denial of the opponent's pair, what the take uncovers and for whom ("uncovers Forum for them", "a face-down card": never a hidden name, tested), play again, Wonder powers that follow, later token effects, and a win. Draft picks, who-starts and the like fall back to a describing tag, so every row has at least one.
- **Expected line:** both Agents got `LineOf(path)` and `ChildrenOf(path)` (read the tree after a search; nothing changes for training). Each row carries its own line (`ExpectedStep`s: player, label, slot, chance step before it); the page shows the hovered row's line (click pins, best by default) under the table with "face-down card revealed" steps, and numbered markers on the pyramid (filled = you, outlined = them). A row whose search didn't get past its own Move (each step needs ≥ 4 visits) shows "No line … (Think longer may help)". A step taking a card that isn't face up now reads "Build a newly revealed card", never a sampled name.
- **Outcome bar:** `NeuralAgent.TrackOutcomes` (set only by the Advisor) averages the leaf outcome-head distributions per node of the first two plies, turned to the searching player's view (terminal leaves count their real class); search results are bit-identical with it on (tested). Shown as a 7-colour stacked bar with legend only when the evaluator has an outcome head (`ILeafEvaluator.HasOutcomeHead`: real ONNX networks; MCTS and `neural:uniform/playout` leave it out). Verified with the committed `tiny.onnx`; a trained network needs nothing more than `--agent neural:<best.onnx>`.
- **Other Pending Decisions:** tiles above a dimmed pyramid with % badges and ranked in the same table (tiles already existed). The Statue of Liberty keep is now **6 keep/give pair tiles and rows**: pair share = keep share × give's share of visits under that keep (tested against a direct search), win = the give node's value; a pair is entered as one change (`POST /api/entry {line, then}`, `AdvisorSession.Enter(lines)`: both or neither). Rows have a `Key` (line, or both lines) used by the share map.
- **Flaky test found and fixed:** `AdvisorStore.WriteAtomically` replaces `current.log` with `File.Move(overwrite)`. On Windows that fails with "Access denied" when another program (virus scanner, search indexer) briefly has the just-written file open; the server didn't catch it, so the entry got an empty 500 and the test a JSON parse error. Reproduced once in this ticket's first full run (`The_page_and_API_…`, empty answer to an entry) and deterministically with a test that holds the file open. Now the write retries for ~0.7 s, and a save that still fails becomes a notice instead of a server error. Also: test servers bind port 0 (no free-port race), the advice-log check reads with shared access, and empty answers fail with their status. 10 Advisor-suite reruns under load: all green.
- **Verified in a browser:** headless Edge over the DevTools protocol against three running servers: an Age II position with MCTS (5 rows, tags on each, line "Build Customs House → face-down card revealed → They: Discard Barracks" with markers 1/2 on the board; hovering a low-share row shows "No line"; Think longer extends the line), the same position with `neural:tiny.onnx` (outcome bars + legend, a 7-step line through two chance steps), and a Statue keep (6 pair tiles over the dimmed board, badges, best pair in gold; clicking its tile logged `liberty-keep` and `liberty-give`). No page errors.
